# Interview guide: first-time eBook creators

**Goal:** understand how people with no writing or design background think about creating an eBook, where they would get stuck, and what would make them proud to share the result.

**Who to talk to (3–5 people, 20 min each):**
- 1–2 people who have *thought about* making an eBook / guide / lead magnet but never did (coach, freelancer, small business owner, creator)
- 1 person who has used an AI writing tool (ChatGPT, Canva, Gamma) to make a long document
- 1 complete novice (no AI tools, no design tools)

**Format:** video call or in person. Record (with permission) or take notes. Part 3 works best with a quick prototype or a competitor (e.g. Gamma or Designrr free trial) on screen.

---

## Part 1 — Context (5 min)

1. Tell me a bit about what you do.
2. Have you ever wanted to create a guide, eBook, or long document to share? What was it about? Who was it for?
3. What happened? (If they never did it: what stopped you?)
4. If you had to make one this week, how would you start?

## Part 2 — Expectations of AI (5 min)

5. Have you used AI to write something longer than an email? How did it go?
6. When AI writes something for you, what makes you trust it — or not?
7. Imagine you describe your book idea and an AI writes the whole thing. What's the first thing you'd want to check?
8. What would make you *embarrassed* to share an AI-made book? What would make you *proud*?

## Part 3 — Reactions to the flow (8 min)

Show the flow (prototype, sketch, or competitor tool). Ask them to think aloud.

9. Here's the first screen. What do you think this tool does? What would you do first?
10. You need to describe your book: topic, theme, audience. Is anything here unclear or hard to answer?
11. Generation takes about a minute. What would you want to see or do while you wait?
12. Here's the result. What do you look at first? What would you change?
13. Say one chapter isn't right. What would you expect to be able to do?
14. You're done. How would you share this, and with whom?

## Part 4 — Wrap-up (2 min)

15. If this tool could only do one thing really well, what should it be?
16. Anything we didn't ask that you think matters?
