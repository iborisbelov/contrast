## 3 · Как я сделал (2:40 – 4:50)

**Research** [Tab 3 → «Talking to the client», Tab 4 → прокрутить]
> I asked you who it’s for — anyone — and why people quit these tools. I studied Designrr and other tools, user reviews, AI design guidelines and book covers. Visuals: a moodboard from Dribbble and Behance.

[Tab 3 → таблица «Research → decisions»]
> Five findings → five decisions:
> Design questions before content → no design questions.
> “The AI ignored me” → every change highlighted and undoable.
> Books that sound like AI → the author’s own tip; AI never invents one.
> “Tone” is abstract → pick from sample lines.
> Spinners kill trust → read chapter 1 while the rest is written.

**Process & AI** [Tab 3 → «How it evolved»: карточки 1 и 5]
> Build first, then decide. AI built three clickable flows in an hour — wizard, chat, growing book. I merged them. Four versions of one screen — “one question at a time” won.

[Tab 3 → «How I worked with AI», Tab 5]
> AI in every step: research, prototypes, design audit, React build, real AI.
> Fast: 100 sources and three prototypes in hours.
> Failed: a crash, a retired model, broken mobile layouts — caught by testing, each fix logged, each made the app sturdier.
> My calls: which version wins, what to cut, API key on the server.
